# Growth Partner — internal scope definition

**Internal.** This defines what the $299/month actually obligates the business to do, so
the plan's published promises always have an operational answer behind them. The public
copy lives in `client/src/content/offer.ts` (`carePricing`) and `content/growth.ts`; the
written client agreement governs. Where this file says **[OWNER DECISION REQUIRED]**, the
repository contains no answer and one must not be invented — decide it in
`docs/owner-decisions-required.md` and record it here.

Last reviewed: 2026-08-13.

---

## Included (published, and therefore owed)

**Every month:**

- Hosting, certificates, backups and security/software updates — costs inside the fee
- Uptime and form-delivery monitoring; bug fixes
- Content, service and photo changes on request
- Business information, hours and service area kept true
- Conversion and user-experience improvements
- Measurement, explained in plain English

**Each quarter:**

- One seasonal refresh (4/year): the current season's service moved to the front, with
  matching messaging and call to action. A refresh is not a redesign.

**When needed (bounded):**

- Up to **1** campaign landing page per calendar month
- Up to **2** campaign copy alignments per calendar month
- A/B testing — **1 active test at a time**, only where traffic supports a meaningful
  result; otherwise evidence-based improvement, called what it is
- Allowances do **not** accumulate month to month

**Service level:**

- 24-business-hour response guarantee (see `docs/guarantee-terms.md` for the full
  definition, channels, exclusions and remedy)

## Not included (published exclusions)

- Managing advertising accounts, budgets, bidding or media buying
- Any promise about rankings, leads, traffic, revenue or ROI
- Work on systems the business cannot access
- Third-party outages (hosting provider, registrar, email provider) — response covers
  communication and coordination, not somebody else's uptime

## Standard request vs. large request

A **standard request** is covered by the fee: content/photo/hours/service edits, small
copy changes, the bounded campaign work above, small UX fixes.

A **large request** is quoted in writing before it starts, never billed afterwards:
new pages beyond the campaign allowance, new functionality, integrations, a booking
system, a redesign, a change of brand, anything materially outside the agreed scope.

The published line that governs both: _"Anything larger is quoted before it starts —
never after."_

## Third-party work

Coordinating with the client's other suppliers (ads agency, booking-software vendor,
photographer) is covered as communication; implementing their requests is scoped like
any other request (standard vs. large).

24 business hours (Mon–Fri, 8am–6pm Pacific; weekends and US federal holidays pause the
clock), through the support form or the designated business email. Remedy: that month's
fee waived in full, applied automatically. Full definition: `docs/guarantee-terms.md`.
