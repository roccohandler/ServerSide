# Future performance guarantee — internal roadmap. DO NOT PUBLISH.

**Nothing in this file may appear on the website.** There is currently no evidence base
to responsibly promise any conversion outcome, and the site's credibility position — "I
guarantee what I control" — depends on not pretending otherwise. This file exists so
that, once real client data accumulates, the question can be evaluated with a checklist
instead of a hunch.

---

## What would have to exist first

1. **Real measurement, live.** An analytics provider actually connected (none is today —
   `lib/analytics.ts` is a seam), with call and form conversions verified per client.
2. **A defensible baseline per client.** At least a full quarter of pre-change or
   early-life conversion data on the client's own site, not a borrowed benchmark.
3. **Minimum traffic.** Enough visits during the measurement period for a change to be
   distinguishable from noise. Rule of thumb: hundreds of sessions and dozens of
   conversion events per period, per variant — most local trades will not clear this;
   the guarantee would be _conditional on traffic_, stated up front.
4. **A portfolio of completed projects** whose before/after data (see
   `docs/proof-collection.md`) shows the improvement is repeatable, not anecdotal.

## The mechanism, if it is ever offered

- **Metric:** website conversion rate — qualified enquiries (tracked calls + quote
  requests) ÷ sessions. Never revenue, never leads-as-a-number, never rankings.
- **Baseline:** agreed in writing per client before the work, from their own data.
- **Traffic threshold:** the minimum sessions per measurement period, stated in the
  agreement; periods below it extend the window rather than void the promise.
- **Measurement period:** e.g. 90 days post-launch, then a defined comparison window.
- **Qualified conversion:** defined per client (which actions count), excluding spam,
  duplicates and the client's own traffic.
- **Attribution:** same analytics property, same counting method, before and after.
- **Client-side factors that pause the clock:** ads switched off, phone unanswered
  (call-answer data if available), pricing changes, seasonal collapse, site edits by
  others.
- **Exclusions:** algorithm updates, outages, market shocks, tracking broken by third
  parties.
- **Remedy — work, not cash:** if the agreed metric has not improved past the agreed
  threshold by the end of the period, optimisation continues at no management fee until
  it does. A money-back remedy requires a legal and cash-flow decision the business has
  not made.

## What must never be offered regardless

"10 leads guaranteed" · "20% conversion increase" · "Double your calls" · "5 customers
or your money back" — every one of these promises a number that depends on demand,
pricing and the phone being answered, none of which the website controls.

## Review trigger

Revisit after **5 completed projects** with clean before/after measurement, or 12
months, whichever is later.
